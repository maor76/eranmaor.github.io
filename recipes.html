My Recipe Book

A personal recipe-book app for Android

What the app does

A personal recipe book for the phone: storing and editing recipes, keeping several versions of the same recipe, an ingredient and unit database, collections and tags, search, a cook mode with timers, and turning a photo, a link, a Word file or a PDF of a recipe into structured text.

Privacy in short

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics.
  • Recipes are stored on the device itself.
  • Google Drive backup is optional and goes only to the user's own Drive.
  • There is no application server, and the developer never sees anyone's recipes.
